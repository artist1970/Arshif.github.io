The Archivist's Craft

Context, care and faithful preservation.

ARSHIF helps researchers understand the boundaries between sources, interpretations and present-day accounts without altering historical originals.

01 · Evidence

Trace the source

Locate original records and document where each claim originated. Distinguish primary material from interpretation.

02 · Preservation

Keep the original

Protect historical language and meaning. Store summaries, translations and commentary as distinct derivatives.

03 · Context

Understand the period

Respect cultural, historical and disciplinary context without silently reconciling conflicting records.
